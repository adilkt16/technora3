  • About TECHNORA

    Technora 3.0, the flagship tech fest of the Department of Computer Science and AI & Department of Computer Application at Safi Institute of Advanced Study (Autonomous) College, is set to redefine arts and science tech events. Since its launch on March 22, 2022, Technora has featured tech events, gaming, and expo stalls. This year, it becomes the first tech fest of its kind in an autonomous arts and science college, rivaling top engineering colleges. With 16 events, 4 workshops, 10+ stalls, and 5,000+ external attendees, Technora 3.0 aims to inspire, innovate, and set new standards for inclusivity and excellence.
