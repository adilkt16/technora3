  • About TECHNORA

    Technora 3.0, the flagship intercollegiate tech fest organized by the Department of Computer Science and AI & Department of Computer Application at Safi College (Autonomous), is set to redefine the standards of tech events in the arts and science domain. Since its inception on March 22, 2022, Technora has showcased a dynamic mix of tech events, gaming experiences, and expo stalls. This year, it scales new heights, becoming the first tech fest of its kind hosted by an autonomous arts and science college, rivaling the grandeur of leading engineering colleges. With 16 tech-driven events, 4 expert-led workshops, 10+ stalls featuring food, fun, and cultural experiences, and an anticipated participation of 5,000+ external attendees, alongside the college’s 2,500-strong student body, Technora 3.0 promises to inspire, innovate, and set a new benchmark for inclusivity and excellence.
